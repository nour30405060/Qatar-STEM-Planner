STEM Lesson Plan Quality Rubric - 100 Points

1. Curriculum Alignment - 10 points
The lesson plan should clearly align with the grade level, science curriculum, unit, lesson topic, and learning outcomes.

2. Science Content Grounding - 10 points
The plan should be grounded in the uploaded textbook lesson pages and should avoid unrelated or generic content.

3. STEM Integration - 15 points
The plan should integrate Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics in a meaningful and connected way.

4. Engineering Design Process - 15 points
The plan should include a clear engineering design process such as Ask, Imagine, Plan, Create, Test, and Improve.

5. Qatar Local Context - 10 points
The plan should connect learning to a relevant local context in Qatar such as water, energy, desert environment, marine environment, food security, health, sustainability, or community needs.

6. SDG Connection - 5 points
The plan should connect meaningfully to one or more Sustainable Development Goals where appropriate.

7. Assessment Plan - 10 points
The plan should include formative and summative assessment methods, with clear criteria for evaluating student learning and STEM performance.

8. Differentiation and Inclusion - 10 points
The plan should include support for struggling students and extension for high-achieving or gifted students.

9. Resources, Safety, and Time Management - 5 points
The plan should identify required materials, technology, safety issues, and realistic timing.

10. Teacher Creative Adaptation - 10 points
The plan should include space for teacher judgment, adaptation, alternative resources, and modifications based on classroom reality.

Total: 100 points
