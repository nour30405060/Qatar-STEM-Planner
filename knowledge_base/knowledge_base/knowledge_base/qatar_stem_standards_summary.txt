Qatar STEM Planning Knowledge Base

This knowledge base supports the AI tool in generating STEM lesson plans that are curriculum-grounded, standards-based, and contextually relevant to Qatar.

Core STEM lesson planning principles:

1. Curriculum Alignment
The STEM lesson plan should be clearly aligned with the science lesson topic, grade level, unit, and learning outcomes.

2. Science Content Grounding
The plan should be based primarily on the uploaded textbook lesson pages and should not generate unrelated or generic content.

3. STEM Integration
The plan should explicitly integrate Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics in a meaningful way.

4. Engineering Design Process
The plan should include an engineering design challenge or design-thinking process where appropriate:
Ask, Imagine, Plan, Create, Test, Improve.

5. Inquiry-Based Learning
The lesson should encourage students to ask questions, investigate, analyze evidence, and explain their reasoning.

6. Problem-Based Learning
The lesson should be organized around a real-world problem, task, or challenge.

7. Local Qatar Context
Where appropriate, the lesson should connect to Qatar contexts such as water, energy, sustainability, desert environment, marine environment, food security, transportation, recycling, or health.

8. Sustainable Development Goals
Where relevant, the plan should connect to selected SDGs such as:
SDG 3 Good Health and Well-being
SDG 4 Quality Education
SDG 6 Clean Water and Sanitation
SDG 7 Affordable and Clean Energy
SDG 11 Sustainable Cities and Communities
SDG 12 Responsible Consumption and Production
SDG 13 Climate Action
SDG 14 Life Below Water

9. Assessment
The plan should include formative and summative assessment, including clear criteria or a rubric.

10. Differentiation
The plan should include support for struggling students and extension for high-achieving students.

11. Resources and Safety
The plan should identify required materials, available resources, classroom constraints, safety considerations, and time management.

12. Teacher Creative Adaptation
The teacher should be able to adapt the AI-generated draft based on student needs, classroom realities, available resources, and professional judgment.

13. Quality Evaluation
The tool should evaluate the plan using a standards-based rubric and provide an overall quality score, sub-scores, strengths, weaknesses, and improvement suggestions.
